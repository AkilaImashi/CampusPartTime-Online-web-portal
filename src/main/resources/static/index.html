How it works

Three roles, one board

Shiftboard doesn't try to be a full job market — it's sized for a campus community: quick one-day gigs and steady part-time roles, posted by real local employers.

1. Set up your portfolio

University, skills, bio, and whether you're free for one-day work — fill it in once.

2. Browse or post

Students filter listings by type; employers write a title, pay, date and location.

3. Track the outcome

Applications move from pending to interview, selected, or rejected — visible to both sides.
